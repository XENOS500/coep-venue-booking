Using the service

Terms of use

Effective date: 17 October 2026

COEP Spaces helps users discover campus venues, check availability and submit requests to assigned Approvers. These terms describe the application's account and booking rules. Questions can be sent to pareekayush500@gmail.com.

Accounts and access

Provide accurate account information and use an email address you control. Requester self-registration requires email verification. Registration grants the ability to submit requests, not an automatic right to use a venue. Approver access is granted by the Admin and is limited by current venue assignments.

Keep your password and verification codes private. Do not share accounts or impersonate another person or authority. Contact the maintainer if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

Venue requests and decisions

Submit accurate event, contact and scheduling details. Requests must satisfy the booking window, capacity, venue configuration and availability rules enforced by the application. Pending requests hold the requested slots while awaiting a decision; a pending request is not an approval.

Assigned Approvers may approve or reject requests. Check your booking status before making arrangements or using a venue. Account creation or an OTP email does not confirm a booking. Venue use also remains subject to applicable campus rules and directions from the responsible authorities.

Changes and cancellation

Use the cancellation options available in your account before the booking's first occurrence begins. Recurring requests are handled as a series under the application's rules. For changes that the interface does not support, contact the responsible authority rather than creating conflicting or misleading requests.

Acceptable use

Do not submit fraudulent requests, harass others, send unwanted verification emails, bypass account or venue permissions, attempt to obtain another user's private information, or interfere with the service. Accounts may be disabled by the authorized administrator or maintenance team when access needs to be withdrawn or misuse is investigated.

Privacy and availability

Review the Privacy policy for details about account information, booking records, browser sessions and Gmail API email delivery. Supply only information needed for your request and avoid including unrelated sensitive information in event descriptions.

The service can be interrupted by maintenance, hosting startup delays, network issues or email-provider limits. Check the application for the current booking status and contact the responsible authority about time-sensitive venue arrangements.

Updates and support

Changes to these terms will be published here with an updated effective date. For account support or questions about using the application, email pareekayush500@gmail.com. Do not include passwords or verification codes.
