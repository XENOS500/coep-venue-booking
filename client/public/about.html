Campus venue booking

A space for your next idea.

COEP Spaces brings venue discovery, availability checks and booking requests into one place. Find a suitable campus venue and send your event request to the people responsible for reviewing it.

Sign in to your workspaceRegister as Requester

Find a venue

Sign in to browse the venue catalog, view available room details and photos, and check dates and times before requesting a space.

Request a booking

Provide your event and contact details, choose a single date or recurring schedule, and track the request in your account.

Review and manage

Assigned Approvers review venue requests. The Admin manages venue configuration, Approver accounts and venue assignments.

Email verification, with a clear purpose

Requesters can register using Gmail, Yahoo, college mail or another valid email address. A verification code confirms access to the address before the account is created. A request still needs an assigned Approver's decision; registration does not grant a venue reservation.

COEP Spaces uses Gmail API to send verification codes and booking approval or rejection notifications from an authorized application sender account. Booking decisions are emailed to the contact address provided in the booking form. Only the sender authorizes Google access. Requesters do not grant access to their inbox, contacts or Google account.

Learn how account, booking and email information is handled in our Privacy policy, and review our Terms of use.
